We don't use advertising, ad tracking or third-party analytics, and we don't sell, rent or share your data for marketing. We don't use your data to train, evaluate or improve AI models.

How we use your Garmin data

When you connect Garmin Connect, Pacelife receives, through Garmin's official APIs, the data you allow in your Garmin Connect account: activities (Activity API), daily health and sleep summaries (Health API), menstrual cycle data only if you opt in (Women's Health API), and we write planned workouts (Training API) and routes (Courses API) to your account. We use this data only to provide Pacelife's features to you: your training history and statistics, your plan and how it adapts, pace and race-time predictions, recovery and readiness guidance, and sending workouts to your watch. Garmin data is linked to your Pacelife account so we can show it to you; it is never shown to other users, sold, or shared with any third party except the processors listed below that host the service. You can stop sharing at any time by disconnecting Garmin in Pacelife (Settings → Account) or in Garmin Connect (Settings → Connected Apps); stored Garmin sign-in tokens are then deleted.

AI transparency statement

Pacelife's training calculations (plans, predictions, recovery and coaching messages) are rule-based formulas that run inside our own service. Pacelife itself sends no data to any AI system.

Claude connector (optional, off by default). In Settings → Claude you can choose to connect Pacelife to Claude, the AI assistant made by Anthropic, so you can ask it about your running. It only works after you turn it on, which creates a private link that you add to your own Claude account. While it's on, when you ask Claude a question it can read, but not change: your training plan, your runs (date, distance, time, pace, heart rate, elevation, splits, laps and best efforts, including runs from Garmin and Apple Health and your imported Strava archive), and Pacelife's fitness estimates, predicted race times and paces. It doesn't include runs received through Strava's API, your health readings (sleep, HRV, resting heart rate), your calendar or your account details. Data is sent only when Claude asks for it in a conversation you start, and Anthropic processes it under its own terms and privacy policy and your Claude account settings; Pacelife doesn't use it to train AI models. You can turn the connector off at any time in Settings → Claude with one tap, which stops the link working immediately.

Where it's stored and who processes it

  • Cloudflare hosts the app and stores each account's data in its own isolated store (Cloudflare Workers and Durable Objects). Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Google provides your calendar events if you connect Google Calendar; it receives no Garmin or health data from us.
  • Garmin and Strava provide your activity and health data when you connect them, under their own terms and privacy policies; you can revoke access at any time from Pacelife or from their settings.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple and app distribution.
  • Open-Meteo receives approximate coordinates for weather forecasts.
  • Anthropic (Claude) receives the running data described above only if you turn on the Claude connector and ask Claude about it.

Health data, and cycle data in particular, is special category data under the GDPR: we process it only with your explicit consent (given when you connect a service or switch a feature on) and only to provide those features to you. Data from Garmin is processed only by Cloudflare, as our hosting provider, and used only to provide Pacelife's features to you. It is not sold, and it is not passed to any other third party, integration partner, technology vendor, analytics or AI service, except to Claude if you turn on the Claude connector yourself (see the AI transparency statement).

How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. Delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete account) and your data is erased from our store straight away; disconnecting Garmin or Strava stops new data and removes the stored sign-in. Copies on your own devices are removed when you delete the app.

Your rights

You can see and correct your data in the app at any time, export it (email hello@pacelife.run and we will send you a complete copy in a machine-readable format, JSON, within one month), and delete it yourself in the app (Settings → Delete account). Under the GDPR you can also ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, object to or restrict how it's used, and withdraw consent (for example by disconnecting a service). Email hello@pacelife.run; we'll reply within one month. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland (dataprotection.ie) or the data protection authority where you live.

Our legal basis is performing our service for you (your account, plan and insights) and your consent for connected services such as Garmin, Strava and your calendar.

Health information

Pacelife gives training information, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new training plan if you have any health concerns.

Children

Pacelife is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
