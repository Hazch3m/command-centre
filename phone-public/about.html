Running plans that adapt to how your training actually goes.

Pacelife builds a training plan for your race from your own running, sends each session to your watch as a structured workout, and adjusts the plan as the weeks go by: easing off when you miss sessions, stepping up when you're thriving.

Plans from your data

Pick a race and date; paces, mileage and sessions come from your recent runs and fitness.

Workouts on your watch

The week ahead goes to your watch's calendar automatically, and updates when the plan changes.

Adapts as you train

Missed sessions, slower or faster paces, plateaus: the plan check suggests the right change.

Insight that matters

Race readiness, predicted times, training load and focus, heart-rate zones and recovery.

Pacelife is in private testing on iPhone. It works with Garmin and Strava, and your data is used only to coach you: it is never sold or shared for advertising.
